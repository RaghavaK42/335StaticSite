Can Four Attack Types Hit Over 1,000 Pokemon?

In Pokemon, a Pokemon can only learn 4 moves at a time, meaning they can only deal damage with four types at a time (under most circumstances). Ona random Thursday about a year ago, a friend asked me in converstation whether it was possible to choose only four attacking types that could hit more than 1,000 Pokemon for super-effective damage. I decided that the most reasonable response was to write a Python program and test every possible type combination.

What does super effective mean?

Every Pokemon has one or two types, and each attack also has a type. An attack is super effective when its type has an advantage over the defending Pokemon's type. For example, Water attacks are super effective against Fire Pokemon. The goal is to determine if it was possible for a Pokemon with 4 different types of coverage to hit over 1000 Pokemon for super effective damage.

My data included 1,215 Pokemon and forms through the Scarlet and Violet DLC. This means the new Mega Evolutions from Legends ZA were not included because my data file I found online didn't have them and I was too lazy to add them myself, so they are left as an exercise for the reader. The Initial Answer

Under the normal type rules, the answer is no. The best combination was Electric, Fighting, Ground, and Flying, which covered 966 Pokemon. That left it 34 Pokemon short of the 1,000-Pokemon goal.

But What About Freeze-Dry?

Freeze-Dry is a strange Ice-type attack because it is super effective against Water Pokemon. Normally, Ice attacks are resisted by Water types. I repeated the calculations as if one of the four types was Freeze-Dry instead of a normal Ice attack.

Final Answer

Four normal attacking types cannot hit more than 1,000 of the Pokemon in this data set for super-effective damage. However, if we use Freeze-Dry as one of the four moves, then several combinations reach 1,000.

The best combination was Ice, Fighting, Ground, and Ghost, which covered 1,055 of the 1,215 Pokemon. I was actually not expecting Ghost to appear in the top spot, nor bug at all, but was pleasantly surprised to see that they did.
